Government are the institutions and procedures through which people are ruled. It primarily exists to prevent chaos. Some of its jobs include:
- Creates and enforces laws
- Creates money
- Provides goods that wouldn't exist (e.g., military) or cost too much (e.g., mail)
- Regulates markets
- Protects civil liberties

The U.S. government is a polity—rule by many. The other *types of government* are monarchy and aristocracy. Our government is organized into offices, and each possess ==authority and responsibility==. Authority is basically legitimacy, while responsibility is the only goals the office must strictly work towards.

Politics is conflictual. Topics of economy, culture, demographics, and ideology are the biggest sources. It is very important to compromise in the name of progress. Liberty encourages healthy discussion and rightful criticism of the government. Similarly, equality notes that everyone is the same in front of the law.



# {{< heading "Collective Action" >}}
Collective action allows a group to achieve greater things than they would have individually. However, there are two ideas that cast doubt on its success.

**Free riders** don't contribute but reap the same rewards as everyone else. One countermeasure is by offering selective or material benefits—exclusive incentives. However, this is not a foolproof solution as the incentive could just simply not be appealing. A better solution is to play into the fact that people want to be with similar people, or have a goal everyone is excited to work towards.

**The Tragedy of the Commons** is an issue of resource overuse. The resource will eventually deplete, which could spell the end of the population. Privatization can help by putting a paywall, blocking out the poor. Another solution is the government regulating the use of the resource. 



# {{< heading "Texas Creed" >}}
The American Creed lists out the big ideas that make up America:
- Individualism: each person should be able to do what they want to do 
- Liberty: government cannot infringe on a person's individual rights
- Constitutionalism: there exists a written document that limits government 
- Equality
- Democracy 

Texas places bigger emphasis particularly on **individualism and liberty** (because its Protestant settlers back in the day liked to be left alone).
