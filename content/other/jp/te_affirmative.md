title: Conjoining Affirmative Clauses

params: 
    desc: て-form allows one to conjoin clauses. With affirmative clauses, there are particular uses of て-form over separate sentences. 
    author: Andrew Nguyen 
---



Conjugating with **て-form** allows one to **conjoin clauses** to the effect of "and" or "then." As a side effect, duplicate particles may appear in one sentence and overall tense is dictated by the final clause.

With ==affirmative clauses==, there are particular reasons to pick て-form over separate sentences. Perhaps it is to create the meaning that whatever transpired in the first clause led to the second clause. 

Clauses with *verbs as predicates* have special て-form functions. When the *subject is constant* across clauses, actions always occur in the same order as they appear. For *different subjects*, the actions are happening in tandem. Alternatively, the action of the first clause is the means or method to the second clause.

{{% example_jp
    jp="「皿洗い機に塩を入れてタコのぬめりを取りました。」"
    en="\"*I put salt in the dishwasher **to** remove the sliminess from the octopus*.\""
%}}

One may want to apply multiple attributes to a noun all at once. To do this, simply follow an attribute in て-form with another attribute. ==The attributes can even be clauses==.

{{% example_jp
    jp="「東京は**賑やかで面白い**。」"
    en="\"*Tokyo is **lively and interesting***.\""
%}}

{{% example_jp
    jp="「**風が強くて**寒い日。」"
    en="\"*A cold day in which **the wind is strong***.\""
%}}

Not a nuance, but if the clauses have an *identical structure with the same verb in the predicate*, the verb and single case particle just before it, if present, can be deleted from every clause *except the final one*.
1. 「ランス君はフランス**へ行って**、セス君は日本**へ行って**、サム君は中国**へ行きました**。」
2. 「ランス君はフランス、 セス君は日本、サム君は中国**へ行きました**。」



# {{< heading "Basic" >}}
{{< standalone_l type="ul" >}}
    - **Copula verb**: で
    - **Adjectives**: ～くて
    - **Verbs**: with basic past, replace ～た (or ～だ) with ～て (or ～で) 
{{< /standalone_l >}}

If a verb is immediately followed by another verb, both will share the same "inputs" (e.g., subject and objects). This can create some emphatic statements.

{{% example_jp
    jp="「真意を見て取る。」"
    en="\"*To grasp the real sense*.\""
%}}



# {{< heading "Polite" >}}
Adjectives are excluded due to their complexity.
- **Copula verb**: でして
- **Verbs**: with polite past, replace ～た with ～て

When trying to apply attributes, the polite て-form cannot be used if the attribute is a clause.
