The goal is to reconstruct the 3D scene from a 2D image. The challenge is that a projected point could be generated from any of the points on the projection ray (single-view ambiguity). 

One potential solution is omitting light, but it is dangerous. Alternatively, triangulate the exact point with two cameras and where the rays intersect. Instead of stereo, just move. If you didn't take the picture, then the 3D scene can be derived from pictorial cues: shading, perspective effects, and familiar objects.

Some geometry problems include:
- Calibration: figure out intrinsic properties of the camera \(K\) that can be used to derive rays
- Recovering structure
- Stereo/Epipolar geometry: given two cameras, find the original point
- Motion: figure out the displacement between cameras
