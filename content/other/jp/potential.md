The potential form refers to the capability one could exhibit something. ==Only volitional verbs are valid==. The basic non-past conjugations are given below. Like ichidan verbs, the final ～る can be replaced to get tenses and politeness.
- Ichidan: ～られる
- Godan: ～める
- 来る: こられる
- する: できる
- Adjectives: ～くいられる
- Predicative adjectival nouns: でいられる

    Remember, godan verbs conjugate based on the final mora. What's provided here is the M-type.
{{< /subtext >}}

# {{< heading "Patterns" >}}
It's common not to conjugate verbs in the potential form. Instead, take the unconjugated verb and follow it with ～ことができる. A lot of する verbs must use this instead of just できる. する verbs with a single character may alternatively use ～せる. する verbs that become voiced may alternatively conjugate like an ichidan verb.

By taking the polite non-past conjugation and replacing the ～ます with ~はしない, it conveys a strong inability to do the thing. To say that you wanted to do something but can't, get the unconjugated verb, attach に, then add the same verb again conjugated in the negative.
