Tension between the U.S. and Venezuela has existed for several decades, but it has escalated with the second Trump administration. He deemed Venezuela a national security threat because of to the number of American deaths due to drug overdose. This line of thinking opens the door for other military actions in other places. 

It started with diplomatic threats and economic sanctions then American attacks against drug boats in the Caribbean. ==Then Venezuelan President Maduro was captured== without Congressional approval. Maduro's government was kept intact, though.

That is not to say that the Venezuelan government was very friendly to the U.S. In fact, the opposition leader may be a better choice for president than the vice-president, who would end up getting promoted. Maduro was corrupt and there are many institutional issues in the government. Things can get worse too as the capture of Maduro opens the door for a Venezuelan civil war, forcing the migration of Venezuelan refugees into potentially the U.S., counteracting Trump's anti-immigration policy.

The Venezuela crisis marks the beginning of the "Donroe Doctrine," an homage to the Monroe Doctrine at building a U.S. sphere of influence in the Americas. Venezuela has enormous untapped oil reserves that, if the U.S. can take control over it, will decrease the influence of other major oil producers, make oil consumers more dependent on the U.S., and the increase in global oil supply will bring down the price of gasoline (tackling Trump's biggest domestic problem of inflation and affordability). However, it is expensive to rebuild the neglect infrastructure and military presence is a must. Of course, there's also the big elephant in the room that is ethics.

The international state has now seen a new level of aggression get set, potentially justifying China in a potential invasion of Taiwan. The international system against military force in foreign countries, that the U.S. built, is also shaken up. If the Venezuelan situation goes really bad, President Trump, his administration, and the Republican Party will be held responsible.
