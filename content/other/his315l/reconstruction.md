Reconstruction encompasses the 12 years after the Civil War. It brought the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendment. The 14th Amendment in particular gives birthright citizenship. This came about to shut down the Supreme Court from stripping black Americans' rights. The 14th Amendment also required states to respect the Bill of Rights. The 15th Amendment gave voting rights to black men.

However, for nearly a century after the Civil War, black Americans continued to be exploited and restricted. Not much improvement over slavery. Sharecropping, Crop Lien System, convict lease. Reconstruction could not solve the deeply rooted racism problem that even existed in the North. Black codes in Southern states also denied black Americans the right to purchase or rent land.

Assassinations do not often create effects that the assassinator wanted. What effect does happen is huge, though. The assassination of President Lincoln radicalized the North. The conspiracy was that the assassination was ordered by high-ordering officers of the Confederacy. Additionally, Andrew Jackson, Lincoln's vice president, was a major racist, angering the North even more. The South also had more power because black Americans now constitute a full person rather than 3/5ths.

Congress attempted to solve the issue by requiring Southern states to ratify the Reconstruction amendments in order to regain their seats in Congress. They also sent the military to the South to ensure the rights of black Americans, but the military was small. Nor could Congress eliminate the slaveholding class and guarantee the rights of black Americans because it would be too radical. 

President Jackson wanted to replace his cabinet with former Confederates, but Congress passed a law that prevented him from firing existing cabinet officials. Jackson was impeached, but not removed from office by one vote.

Then the first black Americans made it to Congress. They did a lot of great things, but white terrorist groups like the Ku Klux Klan overthrew them. Ulysses Grant attacked the KKK, but no president since did. It would be half a century until another black American came to Congress.

In the 1876 Presidental Election, Democrat candidate Samuel Tilden won the popular and electoral vote. Republicans disputed the result and won, giving the win to nominee Rutherford Hayes. Another civil war could brew, but the two parties reached the Compromise of 1877. Hayes would be president, but the military would be removed from the South. Additionally, the South would be required to vote with the North, and the North would fund and control the economy of the South.
