params: 
    desc: は marks the topic of the sentence.

**は** marks what **the sentence is about**. To qualify as a topic, the entity must be registered information—common sense or established with が in a prior sentence. The topic is frequently omitted when it can be inferred. 

<!-- TODO: is は pronounced like /wa/ for every function? -->
{{< subtext >}}
    は is not a case particle.

    は is pronounced /wa/ when used as a topic marker.

    If the topic is also the subject, は absorbs が.
{{< /subtext >}}

は may also carry the meaning of **"at least."** ～くらい or ～ぐらい may immediately attach onto the entity to emphasize.



Fundamentally, は **emphasizes the entity over other related options**. Multiple は's may appear in a sentence for this purpose.

    jp="「本当**は**嬉しいです。」"
    en="\"*I'm actually happy* **(, no matter how I appear)**.\""
